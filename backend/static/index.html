Weekly Alerts

Dry spells, heavy rain, heat, fires near the farms and crop drops, across all your farms: here, and by email every week if you ask.

Verification Report

A numbered PDF for a lender, insurer or buyer: the farm's measured area, its crop seasons from the satellites, rainfall against normal and, where the land is on GeoEstate Land Registry, its title status. Anyone holding it can check it is genuine.

Site Suitability

Soil (FAO HWSD) + weather history (Open-Meteo) → which crops fit this farm, when to plant, and what limits them. Screening-grade: confirm with a soil test.
